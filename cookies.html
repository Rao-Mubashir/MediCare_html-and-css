Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 3, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit MediCare. They help us provide you with a secure healthcare experience by remembering your pharmacy preferences and clinical details.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Health Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the platform to function securely. They enable core functionality such as:

  • Security and pharmacist authentication
  • Secure shopping basket for medicines
  • Clinical order processing
  • Medical profile management

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow us to remember your preferences, such as your saved pharmacy location and primary delivery address.

Compliance & Safe Usage

We use cookies to ensure that only authorized users can access sensitive health information and to maintain the integrity of our pharmacy services.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may prevent you from placing medical orders safely.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at privacy@medicare.com
