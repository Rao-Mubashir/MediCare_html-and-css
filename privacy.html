Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MediCare collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your health and personal information when you use our healthcare and pharmacy platform. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data security and patient confidentiality.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Delivery address and secure payment information
  • Medical prescriptions and order history
  • Communications with our expert pharmacists and support team

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Verify and process your medical prescriptions
  • Deliver your medicines and health supplies safely
  • Send order status updates and delivery notifications
  • Respond to medical inquiries and customer service requests
  • Improve our pharmaceutical services and user experience

Data Security

We implement hospital-grade technical and organizational measures to protect your medical and personal information against unauthorized access, ensuring HIPAA compliance and full encryption of sensitive data.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our data protection officer at privacy@medicare.com
