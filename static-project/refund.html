Refund Policy

Last updated: January 3, 2026

Overview

At MediCare, we prioritize your health and safety. Due to medical regulations, our refund policy is strictly enforced to maintain pharmaceutical integrity.

Eligible Refunds

You may be eligible for a full refund if:

  • You received the incorrect medication or dosage
  • Items reached you in a damaged or compromised state
  • Healthcare supplies are past their expiration date upon delivery
  • Your order was never delivered due to our logistics error

Refund Process

To initiate a refund for health supplies:

  1. Contact our pharmacy support within 48 hours of delivery
  2. Provide the batch number and order ID
  3. Submit clear photos of the damaged or incorrect items
  4. Keep the original packaging for verification

Non-Refundable Items

For safety reasons, the following are non-refundable:

  • Prescription medications once they leave the pharmacy (unless error occurred)
  • Temperature-sensitive medications (insulin, etc.)
  • Hygiene-related products if the seal is broken
  • Change of mind after order dispatch

Contact Us

For refund requests, please email refunds@medicare.com or call our support line.
